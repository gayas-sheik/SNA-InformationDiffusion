PHASE 6 — INTEGRATED ANALYSIS & FINDINGS
======================================================================

Research Question:
How does information spread across social networks, and what factors determine how far, how fast, and through whom it spreads?

======================================================================

Finding 1 — Network Structure
The interaction network contains approximately 10,000 users connected through 48,463 unique interaction edges. The network is sparse, with a density of approximately 0.000485. Despite this sparsity, the largest weakly connected component contains approximately 9,421 users, indicating that most users belong to one large connected region of the interaction network.

Finding 2 — User Influence
Influence differs substantially across user types. The user type with the highest mean influence score is Influential, with an average influence of 0.805. The same type has a mean activity score of 0.778.

Finding 3 — Centrality and Influence
User influence is positively associated with multiple network-centrality measures. The strongest meaningful observed relationship is between influence score and PageRank, with a correlation of 0.7014. This suggests that users occupying structurally important positions in the synthetic network tend to have higher influence scores.

Finding 4 — Information Cascades
The analysis identifies 1,876 information cascades. The average cascade contains 26.65 interactions, with a median of 28 and a maximum of 75. The average cascade depth is 26.65, while the deepest cascade reaches 75 levels. Average cascade duration is approximately 249.55 hours.

Finding 5 — Cross-Platform Diffusion
Cross-platform diffusion is widespread in the synthetic network. 1,729 of 1,876 cascades involved cross-platform propagation (92.16%). These cascades had an average size of 27.54 interactions, compared with 16.26 interactions for cascades without cross-platform diffusion. The platform transition results show that information can propagate across all five platforms.

Finding 6 — Cross-Community Diffusion
Cross-community diffusion is also extensive. 1,822 of 1,876 cascades involved multiple communities (97.12%). These cascades had an average size of 27.13 interactions, compared with 10.63 interactions for cascades without cross-community diffusion. This shows that simulated information propagation is not restricted to a single community.

Finding 7 — Temporal Activity
Interaction activity varies across the six-month period. The highest monthly activity occurs in 2026-06, with 9,463 interactions, while the lowest occurs in 2026-01, with 6,570 interactions.

Finding 8 — Daily Activity Pattern
The highest simulated interaction activity occurs at hour 23:00, with approximately 2,568 interactions.

Finding 9 — Weekly Activity Pattern
Tuesday has the highest simulated interaction volume, with 8,564 interactions.

Finding 10 — Propagation Rate
The monthly propagation rate remains relatively stable across the observation period, averaging 41.83%. The highest monthly rate is 43.49% in 2026-05.

Finding 11 — Overall Diffusion Pattern
The combined results indicate that information propagation in the synthetic social network is shaped by several interacting dimensions: network connectivity, user influence, temporal activity, cross-platform movement, and cross-community movement. Highly connected and influential users occupy important structural positions, while information cascades frequently cross platform and community boundaries.

Finding 12 — Dataset and Modeling Limitation
The dataset is a controlled synthetic dataset designed to model information-diffusion mechanisms. Therefore, the findings should be interpreted as structural and simulation-based observations rather than direct claims about real-world social-media behavior. In particular, the generated cascades are predominantly chain-like, which limits interpretation of branching cascade topology.
